HOW IT WORKS

  1. Click Start. You’ll be welcomed, then guided on screen and by voice.
  2. When invited, close your eyes and speak freely about how you feel. Your words appear at the bottom; open your eyes when you’re done.
  3. AI turns your story into personal words, music, and visuals. Follow the cues — including closing your eyes again for the silent meditation.
  4. When asked, move your face to gently release the mandala.
  5. After the final prompt, speak about how you feel now, then click Done.

CONTROLS

  • Profile — your name and sign out
  • Calendar — history of past sessions and charts
  • Volume — music level (double-click to mute)
  • Info — these instructions

For the best experience, use headphones and a quiet, well-lit space.
The webcam reads eye open/close and facial expression for the charts only: no video is recorded or saved.
